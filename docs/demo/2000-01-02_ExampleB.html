How are the scores calculated?

Map the White centipawn evaluation cp to an index: 100 / (1 + exp(−0.00368208 × cp)). For the moving side, take the index drop Δ. Move quality is clamp(103.1668 × exp(−0.04354 × Δ) − 3.1669, 0, 100). No drop scores 100.

Game and phase scores are arithmetic means of the relevant moves. An average can hide one decisive mistake. These are not Chess.com or Lichess game accuracy scores, or your actual winning probability. Based on the Lichess move formula; game aggregation is defined by this plugin.

Index drops of at least 20 / 10 / 5 are labeled blunder / mistake / inaccuracy using this plugin’s thresholds. Mate maps to 100 for the winning side and 0 for the losing side; a rule-based draw maps to 50. Average pawn loss includes only moves with cp scores both before and after; mate is never treated as a huge pawn value.

Phases use a heuristic: endgame when total non-pawn material is at most 13, or at most 26 without queens; otherwise, the first 10 full moves are the opening and later moves the middlegame. Knights/bishops count 3, rooks 5 and queens 9. A phase without moves receives no score.
